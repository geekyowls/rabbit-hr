# Code of conduct

RabbitHR follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/geekyowls/rabbit-hr/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.
